# IORP Gamemode Overview

## Welcome to IORP

IORP is a feature-rich multiplayer roleplay gamemode built for San Andreas Multiplayer (SA-MP), offering a dynamic open-world experience with deep progression, immersive roleplay systems, competitive activities, and a player-driven economy.

Whether you want to build a business empire, join law enforcement, become a criminal mastermind, or simply enjoy the world with friends, IORP provides countless ways to create your own story.

---

# Player Progression

Every player begins their journey by creating a character and gradually progressing through the server.

Features include:

* Secure account system
* Character profiles
* Playtime progression
* Achievements
* Inventory management
* Graduation & specialization
* VIP benefits
* Roleplay progression
* Character customization

---

# Economy

The economy is designed to encourage long-term progression and player interaction.

Players can:

* Open and manage bank accounts
* Store valuables in vaults
* Earn rewards
* Trade resources
* Purchase food and supplies
* Invest in Bitcoin
* Buy vehicles
* Operate businesses
* Farm resources
* Participate in industrial production
* Transport shipments across San Andreas

Every profession contributes to the server's living economy.

# Crime & Law Enforcement

For players seeking high-risk, high-reward gameplay, IORP features a complete criminal ecosystem.

Activities include:

* Illegal weapon trading
* Safe cracking

* Organized mafia activities
* Wanted system
* Police pursuits

Law enforcement officers have access to specialized tools and systems to maintain public order.

# Built for Long-Term Progression

IORP is designed around meaningful progression rather than short-term gameplay. Every system—from jobs and businesses to factions and criminal activities—contributes to a living world where player choices have lasting impact.

Whether your goal is to become a successful entrepreneur, a respected officer, a feared criminal, or simply explore everything San Andreas has to offer, IORP provides a rich and evolving multiplayer experience for every playstyle.
